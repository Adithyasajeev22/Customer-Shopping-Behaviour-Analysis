# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, spending behaviour, product preferences, discounts, ratings, and demographic characteristics.

The project uses **Python, MySQL, Excel, and Power BI** to transform raw customer shopping data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

The main goal of this project is to understand customer behaviour, identify purchasing trends, analyze product performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall customer purchasing behaviour.
- Compare revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Identify high-value customers.
- Analyze customer spending patterns.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze product ratings and customer satisfaction.
- Understand the impact of discounts on customer spending.
- Analyze purchasing behaviour across different categories.
- Analyze customer purchase frequency.
- Identify important customer segments.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.
