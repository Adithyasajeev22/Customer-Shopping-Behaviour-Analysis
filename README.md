## 📌 Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes customer purchasing patterns, product performance, discounts, customer reviews, subscription behaviour, demographics, shipping preferences, and purchasing frequency.

The project uses **Python, MySQL, and Power BI** to transform raw customer shopping data into meaningful business insights and an interactive dashboard.

The main objective is to understand:

- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Revenue contribution
- Product performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Discount usage
- Subscription behaviour
- Age-group purchasing patterns
- Shipping preferences
- Repeat customer behaviour

---

# 🎯 Business Problem Statement

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers’ shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty.

The management team has noticed changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels. They are particularly interested in understanding which factors, such as discounts, reviews, seasons, and payment preferences, influence consumer decisions and repeat purchases.

### Main Business Question

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze customer purchasing behaviour.
2. Identify revenue-generating customer segments.
3. Analyze product and category performance.
4. Understand customer review ratings.
5. Analyze discount usage.
6. Compare subscribed and non-subscribed customers.
7. Analyze customer behaviour by age group.
8. Identify purchasing patterns.
9. Analyze shipping preferences.
10. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
11. Generate actionable business insights.
